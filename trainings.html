Trainings & workshops · Denver and online

Trainings that survive Monday.

Half and full days on the things that come up in consultation over and over, in rooms of fifteen or twenty, taught by clinicians who still see clients on Thursdays. Bring a case. There is time to talk about it.

FormatHalf and full daysIn person in Denver, and online

01

What we teach

The topics come from our own consultation, which is to say from the cases that keep coming back. Each training names its teacher, their license, and what they actually do all week.

  1. i

    Couples work where one person has quietly checked out

    The session is calm. Nobody is fighting. One of them has already left and the other hasn’t noticed. What to do in the room when the problem is the absence of a problem, and how to tell ambivalence from a decision that has already been made.

    Full dayAny license

  2. ii

    The Enneagram, used clinically

    Not as a parlor game and not as a personality test to hand across the desk. As a map of what a client does under threat, what they are protecting, and why the thing that helped last time is making this week worse. For clinicians who already have a working knowledge of the types.

    Full daySome familiarity assumed

  3. iii

    Weight-neutral care

    Health at Every Size informed practice for therapists who did not get this in graduate school: the research, the language, what to do when a client’s doctor is saying the opposite, and how to notice your own stuff before it reaches the client.

    Half dayIn person & online

  4. iv

    Trauma work that has stopped moving

    The client is engaged, the alliance is good, the protocol is right, and nothing has shifted in four months. A day on what stalls look like from the inside, what tends to be underneath them, and how to decide between pushing, pausing, and referring out.

    Full dayBring a case

02

How a day runs

A hotel conference room with a slide deck read out loud is six hours you will not get back. This is the other kind.

  1. i

    The morning is teaching, with the slides doing less work than the examples. The examples are current because the person giving them saw clients this week.

  2. ii

    The afternoon is cases. Yours, if you brought one. The room is small enough that it gets a real conversation rather than a question from the floor.

  3. iii

    You leave with something you can use on Monday, and a few people whose numbers you might actually call.

Online trainings are kept to the same size as the room would be. Private trainings for practices and agencies run the same way, at your site or ours.

03

Before you sign up

Do the trainings carry CE credit?

CE approval is in progress. Once it is confirmed, the hours and the approving body will be listed on each training that carries credit, and on the ones that don’t, we will say so at the top of the page rather than leaving you to find out at the end.

Are they in person or online?

Both, depending on the training. In-person days are in Denver. Online ones are kept to the same size as the room would be, because twenty faces on a screen is a webinar and forty is a broadcast, and neither is what we are doing.

How big is a training?

Fifteen people, maybe twenty. Small enough that you can bring a case and there is time to talk about it, which is the whole reason to come in person rather than watch a recording.

Can you bring a training to our practice?

Yes. We run private trainings for group practices, agencies and clinical teams, at your site or ours. Tell us the size of your team, what you keep running into, and roughly what you have to work with, and we will tell you what is possible.

When are the first dates?

Trainings are being scheduled around the November 2027 retreat and the opening consultation cohort. Dates open to the waitlist first, roughly eight weeks ahead, and the rooms are small enough that they tend not to reach a second announcement.
